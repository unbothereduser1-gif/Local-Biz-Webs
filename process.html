Common Questions

Process FAQs

How long does the entire process take? ▼

Free homepages are delivered in 3-5 business days. Professional websites take 2-3 weeks from start to launch. Enterprise projects with e-commerce typically take 4-6 weeks. We'll give you a specific timeline during your initial consultation based on your project requirements.

What happens after I get my free homepage? ▼

You have three options: keep it as-is at no cost, upgrade to a full website with more pages and features, or walk away—no pressure whatsoever. Many clients love their free homepage so much they upgrade within days, but the choice is entirely yours.

How involved do I need to be in the process? ▼

We need about 2-3 hours of your time total: initial consultation (45 min), design review (30 min), final review (30 min), and training (30 min). We handle all the technical work while keeping you informed at key decision points.

What if I need changes after the website is live? ▼

All our packages include free support for the specified period (1-6 months depending on the plan). After that, you can make changes yourself using the content management system, or we can help on an hourly basis or through support packages.

Do I need to provide content or do you write it? ▼

We offer both options! You can provide your own content, or we can write it for you. Our Professional and Enterprise packages include professional copywriting. For free homepages, we'll help optimize any content you provide.

Can I see examples of websites you've built? ▼

Absolutely! Check out our portfolio page to see real examples of websites we've built for businesses just like yours. We've worked with restaurants, retail stores, professional services, healthcare providers, and more.
