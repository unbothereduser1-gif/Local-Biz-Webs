Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the free homepage really free? ▼

Yes! Absolutely no cost, no credit card required, and no obligation. We build you a professional homepage so you can see exactly what we can do for your business. If you love it and want more pages, we can discuss upgrading to a full website.

How long does it take to build a website? ▼

Free homepages are typically delivered within 3-5 business days. Professional websites take 2-3 weeks, and Enterprise projects take 4-6 weeks depending on complexity. We'll give you a specific timeline during your consultation.

What's included in the monthly pricing? ▼

Monthly plans include hosting, security updates, regular backups, technical support, and content updates (up to 2 hours per month). You'll never have to worry about the technical side of your website.

Can I switch plans later? ▼

Absolutely! You can upgrade from your free homepage to any paid plan at any time. We'll credit any work already done and make the transition seamless. You can also upgrade between Professional and Enterprise plans as your business grows.

Do I own the website? ▼

Yes! With one-time payment plans, you own the website completely. With monthly plans, you own the content and design, but hosting and maintenance are included in your monthly fee. You can always switch to ownership by paying the one-time fee.

What if I need help after the website is built? ▼

All plans include support for the specified duration. After that, you can purchase ongoing support packages or pay as you go for updates. We're always here to help you succeed!
